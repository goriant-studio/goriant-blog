# Privacy Policy for J IDE Lite

Effective date: March 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how J IDE Lite handles information when you use the application.

J IDE Lite is a free Android application designed for local editing and development use. We value privacy and aim to keep the product simple, transparent, and respectful of users.

## 1. Overview

J IDE Lite does not collect personal data from users.

J IDE Lite does not require account registration.

J IDE Lite does not provide services for children under 13 years of age.

J IDE Lite is intended for general users who are at least 13 years old.

## 2. Information We Collect

J IDE Lite does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

This includes, but is not limited to, the following categories:

1. Name  
2. Email address  
3. Phone number  
4. Physical address  
5. Payment information  
6. Location data  
7. Contacts  
8. Photos or videos  
9. Messages  
10. Browsing history  
11. Device identifiers for tracking  
12. Advertising data

## 3. Local File Access

J IDE Lite may allow users to open, read, edit, or save files stored on their own device.

These files are processed locally on the device for app functionality only.

We do not upload user files to our servers.

We do not scan user content for advertising purposes.

We do not use user files for profiling, analytics, or tracking.

## 4. Data Sharing

J IDE Lite does not share personal data with third parties because no personal data is collected by the app.

## 5. Ads and Tracking

J IDE Lite does not use advertising services.

J IDE Lite does not use tracking technologies for user profiling.

J IDE Lite does not sell user data.

## 6. Analytics

J IDE Lite does not use analytics services to collect user behavior data.

If analytics or similar services are added in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those changes are introduced.

## 7. Children’s Privacy

J IDE Lite is not directed to children under 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so that we can review the issue and take appropriate action.

## 8. Data Security

Because J IDE Lite does not collect or store personal data on our servers, the privacy risk is significantly reduced.

However, users remain responsible for protecting their own device, local files, and backups.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect product changes, legal requirements, or operational updates.

When this policy is updated, the revised version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

J IDE Lite is built with a simple privacy approach:
