# **🔊 Dev Log #2 – Snake3D: The Silence is Deafening!**

Hey everyone! 👋

So, Snake3D is alive. The snake slithers. The grid looks slick. The cubes get munched. But you know what’s missing? 

**The *CHOMP*.** 
The *BEEP*. 
The despairing *CRASH* when you inevitably run your majestic 30-block long glowing neon snake directly into a wall. 💥 

Right now, playing Snake3D feels a bit like being in outer space—it looks cool, but no one can hear you scream when you lose your high score. We need to fix that.

---

## **🎵 What's Next: The Audio Overhaul**

For the next update, I’m locking myself in the sound booth (read: my bedroom with a ☕ coffee) to implement a fully-fledged audio system for the game! 

Here is the master plan for upcoming **v1.1 Audio Update**:

### **1. The Core SFX**
- **The Munch:** A satisfying, crispy UI pop every time you grab a food block. Nothing triggers the dopamine receptors quite like a good *nom* sound.
- **The Game Over Crunch:** A dramatic sound effect for those moments when you trap yourself in a corner. 
- **Menu Clicks:** Snappy, tactile feedback so navigating the UI feels like you're hacking a mainframe.

### **2. Dynamic BGM (Background Music)**
I’m hunting for the perfect synth-wave, retro-arcade track. Something that screams "I am a glowing digital entity in a neon void." 
The goal? Create a loop that keeps you pumped without getting annoying after your 50th attempt. 🎧

### **3. Spatial Audio in Three.js? 🤔**
*Maybe... just maybe...* we can experiment with Three.js positional audio. Imagine the sound of the food pulsing and getting louder as you get closer to it in the 3D space! No promises yet, but it’s on the experiment board!

## **🕹️ Stay Tuned!**

I'll be diving deep into the Web Audio API and finding (or making) the perfect sound assets over the next few days. 

Get ready to turn up your volume! Let me know what kind of background music you think fits best—Synthwave? Chiptune? EDM? Leave a comment on the socials!

Until next time, keep your snake away from the walls! 🐍✨
