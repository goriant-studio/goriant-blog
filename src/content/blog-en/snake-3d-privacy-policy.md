# Privacy Policy for Snake 3D

Effective date: March 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Snake 3D handles information when you use the application.

Snake 3D is a free casual game. We value privacy and aim to keep the product simple, transparent, and safe for all users.

## 1. Overview

Snake 3D does not collect personal data from users.

Snake 3D does not require account registration.

Snake 3D does not display advertisements.

Snake 3D is intended for general audiences. It is not directed to children under 13.

## 2. Information We Collect

Snake 3D does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

This includes, but is not limited to:

1. Name  
2. Email address  
3. Phone number  
4. Physical address  
5. Payment information  
6. Location data  
7. Contacts  
8. Photos or videos  
9. Messages  
10. Browsing history  
11. Device identifiers for tracking  
12. Advertising data

## 3. Data Sharing

Snake 3D does not share personal data with third parties because no personal data is collected by the app.

## 4. Ads and Tracking

Snake 3D does not use advertising services.

Snake 3D does not use tracking technologies or user profiling.

Snake 3D does not sell user data.

## 5. Analytics

Snake 3D does not use analytics services to collect user behavior data.

If analytics or similar services are added in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those changes are introduced.

## 6. Children's Privacy

Snake 3D is suitable for general audiences but is not specifically directed to children under 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

## 7. Data Security

Because Snake 3D does not collect or store personal data on our servers, privacy risk is significantly reduced.

Users remain responsible for protecting their own device and local data.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect product changes, legal requirements, or operational updates.

When updated, the revised version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
